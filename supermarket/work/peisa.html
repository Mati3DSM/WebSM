PEISA

Client
Peisa
Year
2019
Services
Campaign, Strategy, Creativity

Peisa, the leading company in the heating and hot water market in Argentina, entrusted us with the challenge of carrying out the relaunching campaign of its portfolio.

For this task, we immersed ourselves in the development of the 'Más Peisa' campaign, a brand positioning and consolidation strategy that sought to highlight the company's trajectory and excellence. We implemented our creativity and design to create a graphic and communication system that was deployed on billboards, digital media and strategic points of sale.

The main objective was to reinforce Peisa's image as an indisputable reference in the market, highlighting its expertise in consulting and the superior quality of its products.

Through a solid and coherent message, we communicate that Peisa is more in every aspect: more quality at the best price, more convenience, more style, more technology and more practicality for home environments. This campaign encapsulated the essence of Peisa and reaffirmed its commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction in every aspect of its offering.

Design and Creativity for PEISA's Leading Heating Brand Campaign for Antarctica.
